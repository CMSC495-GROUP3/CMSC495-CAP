Title: Workplace Health and Safety Policy
Category: Workplace
Owner: Facilities
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Scope

This policy covers every Meridian office and applies to employees,
contractors, and visitors. Remote workers should review the Home Office
Safety section below.

## General office safety

Keep aisles, stairwells, and exits clear of boxes, cables, and equipment.
Report a burned-out exit light, a broken door, or a blocked fire exit to
Facilities the same day you notice it.

Each floor has a designated fire warden, listed on the safety board near the
elevators. Fire drills run twice a year; participation is required for
anyone in the building at the time.

## Reporting an incident

Report any injury, near-miss, or unsafe condition to Facilities and your
manager within 24 hours, using the incident form at meridian.io/safety-report.
A near-miss report carries no negative consequence; the goal is to fix the
hazard before it causes an injury.

Facilities investigates every reported incident and posts a summary of
corrective action to the floor's safety board within 10 business days.

## Medical emergencies

Every floor has at least one AED and a first aid kit, marked with a green
cross sign. Call 911 first for a serious medical emergency, then notify
building security at the front desk so they can direct responders.

Each office maintains a roster of employees certified in CPR and first aid;
the roster is posted next to the AED.

## Ergonomics

Meridian provides a sit-stand desk and an ergonomic chair at every desk.
Request an ergonomic assessment from Facilities if you experience discomfort;
assessments are completed within 5 business days and can result in
additional equipment such as a monitor arm or footrest, provided at no cost.

## Workplace violence

Threats, intimidation, and physical violence are prohibited without
exception. If you feel unsafe, leave the area and contact building security
or 911 immediately, then notify People Operations. See the Workplace
Violence Prevention Policy for the full threat assessment and response
process.

## Weather and building closures

Building closures for weather or other emergencies are announced by 6:00 AM
local time through the company Slack #announcements channel and by text to
your registered phone number. A closure does not reduce your PTO balance;
treat the day as a company holiday for pay purposes.

## Home office safety

Remote employees are expected to maintain a workspace with adequate
lighting, a stable surface for equipment, and clear egress. Meridian is not
responsible for injuries occurring in the home outside of dedicated work
tasks during working hours; a work-related injury at home should still be
reported using the same incident form within 24 hours.
