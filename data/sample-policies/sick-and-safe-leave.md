Title: Sick and Safe Leave Policy
Category: Time Off & Leave
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This fictional Meridian Systems policy explains how employees request and
record short-term sick leave and safe leave against Meridian's combined PTO
bank. It does not create a separate paid-leave bank or a universal statutory
entitlement. Local sick-leave ordinances may impose different floors or
process rules; ask People Operations when your work location has a local
mandate. People Operations—not this document—decides how any jurisdiction-
specific requirement interacts with the Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy.

## Combined bank

Meridian maintains one PTO balance for vacation, personal days, and short-term
illness (see the Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy). Sick leave and safe leave under
this policy are usage categories and process rules against that same balance.
There is no second company accrual bank for sick or safe leave.

## Eligible uses

Accrued PTO may be used, following the notice steps below, for:

- the employee's illness, injury, or preventive care;
- care of a family member with the same needs;
- absences related to domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking that affect
  the employee or a covered family member (safe leave).

## Notice and documentation

When the absence is foreseeable, notify your manager before the shift when
practical. For unplanned illness or safe leave, notify your manager as early as
possible on the day of the absence and record the time in Workday as PTO under
the appropriate sick or safe-leave reason code within 3 business days of
returning. For absences longer than three consecutive workdays, People
Operations may request reasonable documentation. Safe-leave documentation may
be limited to the minimum needed to confirm the need; do not press for incident
details.

## Interaction with other leave

Exhausting available PTO does not automatically approve longer medical leave—
submit a Medical and Family Leave request when the absence will exceed what
short-term PTO covers. Statutory FMLA or state leave rights, when they apply,
are distinct from this company PTO usage process; see the Medical and Family
Leave Policy. Jurisdiction-only questions (local ordinances, protected safe-
leave floors) go to People Operations.
