Title: Sick and Safe Leave Policy
Category: Time Off & Leave
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This fictional Meridian Systems policy covers short-term sick leave and
safe leave as a bank separate from PTO. It does not create a universal
statutory entitlement; local sick-leave ordinances may impose different
floors. Ask People Operations when your work location has a local mandate.

## Accrual and use

Full-time employees accrue 1 hour of paid sick and safe leave for every 30 hours
worked, up to 56 hours per calendar year. Part-time employees accrue on the same
ratio. Accrued leave may be used for:

- the employee's illness, injury, or preventive care;
- care of a family member with the same needs;
- absences related to domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking that affect
  the employee or a covered family member (safe leave).

## Notice and documentation

When the absence is foreseeable, notify your manager before the shift when
practical. For absences longer than three consecutive workdays, People Operations
may request reasonable documentation. Safe-leave documentation may be limited to
the minimum needed to confirm the need; do not press for incident details.

## Interaction with other leave

Sick and safe leave is a separate bank from PTO. PTO covers vacation and personal
days; it is not a combined sick/PTO bank. Exhausting sick leave does not
automatically approve longer medical leave—submit a Medical and Family Leave
request when the absence will exceed what this policy covers. Statutory FMLA
rights, when they apply, are distinct from this company sick bank; see the
Medical and Family Leave Policy.
