Title: 401(k) Retirement Savings Plan
Category: Benefits
Owner: Benefits Team
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Eligibility

All employees aged 21 or older are eligible to participate from their first day
of employment. There is no service waiting period for employee contributions.

The plan is administered by Fidelity. Enroll, change contributions, and manage
investments at netbenefits.com or through the Fidelity app.

## Automatic enrollment

If you take no action within 30 days of your start date, you are automatically
enrolled at a 4% pre-tax contribution rate, invested in the target date fund
closest to your 65th birthday.

Automatic contributions increase by 1% each January until reaching 10%. You may
opt out of automatic escalation, or out of the plan entirely, at any time. If you
opt out within 90 days of your first automatic contribution, you may request a
refund of those contributions.

## Company match

Meridian matches 100% of the first 3% of eligible pay you contribute, then 50%
of the next 2%. Contributing 5% therefore earns a 4% match — the maximum.

The match is calculated and deposited each pay period. There is no true-up at
year end, so front-loading contributions early in the year can cost you match on
later periods. Spread contributions evenly to capture the full match.

## Vesting

Your own contributions are always 100% vested immediately.

Company match vests on a three-year cliff schedule: 0% before three years of
service, 100% at three years. Service is counted from your hire date, including
any period of approved leave.

## Contribution limits

The 2026 IRS elective deferral limit is $24,500. Employees aged 50 and older may
make an additional catch-up contribution of $8,000, and employees aged 60 through
63 may contribute a higher catch-up of $11,250.

Contributions may be pre-tax, Roth, or split between them. The limit applies to
the combined total.

## Investments

The plan offers target date funds, a set of index funds spanning US and
international equity and fixed income, and a self-directed brokerage window.
Default enrollment goes to a target date fund.

Meridian does not provide investment advice. Fidelity offers free planning
consultations to all participants.

## Loans and withdrawals

You may take up to two loans at a time, for the lesser of $50,000 or 50% of your
vested balance, repaid through payroll deduction over up to five years, or up to
fifteen years for a primary residence purchase.

Hardship withdrawals are available for reasons defined by the IRS, including
medical expenses, purchase of a primary residence, tuition, and preventing
eviction. Hardship withdrawals are taxable and may carry a 10% penalty.

## Leaving Meridian

Your vested balance is yours. You may leave it in the plan if it exceeds $7,000,
roll it into an IRA or a new employer's plan, or cash it out — cashing out before
age 59½ generally triggers income tax plus a 10% penalty.

Unvested match is forfeited at separation. If you are rehired within five years,
prior service counts toward the vesting cliff.
