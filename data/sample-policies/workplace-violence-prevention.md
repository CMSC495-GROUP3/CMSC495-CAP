Title: Workplace Violence Prevention Policy
Category: Workplace
Owner: Facilities
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Policy statement

Meridian has zero tolerance for violence, threats of violence, and
intimidating behavior in the workplace, in any Meridian facility, at any
company event, or in work-related communication. This applies to
employees, contractors, vendors, and visitors.

## Prohibited conduct

- Physical assault, or attempted physical assault.
- Threatening language, gestures, or written communication, including on
  Slack or email.
- Bringing a weapon onto company premises, except a law enforcement officer
  acting in an official capacity.
- Stalking or following a coworker.
- Destruction of company or coworker property as an act of intimidation.

## If you feel unsafe right now

Leave the immediate area if you can safely do so, call 911, and then notify
building security at the front desk or, if remote, notify your manager and
People Operations immediately. Do not attempt to physically intervene in an
active threat.

## Reporting a concern

Report a threat, or behavior that concerns you even if it doesn't rise to a
clear threat, to your manager, People Operations, Facilities, or the
anonymous ethics line at meridian.ethicspoint.com. Report as soon as
possible; a delayed report is still taken seriously, but faster reporting
allows a faster response.

You do not need certainty that someone intends harm. A comment like "you'll
regret this" made in anger, or a pattern of behavior that makes a coworker
afraid, is worth reporting.

## Threat assessment

People Operations and Facilities convene a threat assessment within 24
hours of a credible report, and sooner for an active or immediate concern.
The assessment may involve Legal, building security, and outside law
enforcement depending on severity, and can result in actions ranging from a
documented conversation to an immediate suspension pending investigation, a
building access restriction, or a restraining order request.

## Domestic violence spillover

If a personal situation, such as a restraining order against a former
partner, could create a risk at work, tell People Operations. Meridian will
work with you confidentially on options such as a photo alert to building
security, a parking or entrance change, or a temporary remote work
arrangement, without requiring you to disclose more than you're comfortable
sharing.

## Consequences

A substantiated violation results in termination under the Disciplinary
Action and Termination Policy, regardless of role or tenure. Retaliation
against someone who reports a safety concern in good faith is itself a
policy violation.
