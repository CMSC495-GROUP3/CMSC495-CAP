Title: Dress Code and Workplace Appearance Policy
Category: Workplace
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## General standard

Meridian's standard is business casual, with a lot of latitude for
individual style. There is no list of banned items for a typical workday;
use judgment appropriate to what you're doing that day.

## Everyday office and remote work

Jeans, sneakers, t-shirts, and casual tops are fine for a normal day in the
office or on video calls. Clothing with offensive language or imagery is
not appropriate for work, on video or in person.

There is no dress code difference by gender. Choose from any category of
clothing that fits the general standard.

## Client meetings and external events

For a meeting with a customer, partner, or at an external event such as a
conference, dress one level up from your everyday standard, generally
business casual with a more polished edge: a collared shirt, blouse, or
similar. Your manager will flag it in advance if a specific meeting calls
for formal business attire.

## Safety-sensitive areas

Employees or visitors in Facilities, the server room, or the hardware lab
must wear closed-toe shoes. Facilities can provide safety glasses and
other required equipment on request; check with Facilities before your
first visit to these areas.

## Religious and cultural accommodation

Religious and cultural dress, including head coverings, is always
appropriate and does not require prior approval. See the Disability and
Workplace Accommodation Policy if a specific role's safety requirement
conflicts with a religious practice; Facilities and People Operations will
work with you on an alternative.

## Remote video calls

Cameras on for internal meetings is a team-level norm, not a company
requirement. When on camera, follow the same everyday standard above;
background and lighting are your choice.

## Questions

If you're unsure whether something fits the standard for a specific
situation, ask your manager or People Operations before the event rather
than after.
