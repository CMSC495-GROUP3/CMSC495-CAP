Title: Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy
Category: Finance
Owner: Finance
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Principle

Spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own, and be able to
explain any expense to a colleague without discomfort. Where this policy is
silent, use that standard.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in Expensify within 30 days of the transaction. Expenses
submitted more than 60 days after the transaction require director approval and
may be declined.

Receipts are required for any expense of $25 or more. For expenses under $25, a
description is sufficient. Itemized receipts are required for all meals
regardless of amount — a credit card slip showing only a total is not enough.

Reimbursements are paid with the next regular payroll run after approval.
Approved expenses submitted by the 15th are paid at month end.

## Air travel

Book economy class for flights under six hours and premium economy for flights
of six hours or more. Business class requires VP approval.

Book at least 14 days in advance where possible. If you must book inside 7 days,
note the reason on the expense report.

Meridian does not reimburse seat upgrades, priority boarding, or lounge access.
You may keep personal frequent flyer miles and status earned on company travel.

## Lodging

Book standard rooms up to $300 per night in major metro areas and $200 elsewhere.
Where a conference has a room block, book the block rate even if it exceeds these
caps.

## Meals

Daily meal limits while traveling are $85 in major metro areas and $60 elsewhere,
inclusive of tax and tip. These are limits, not per diems — claim what you spent.

Alcohol is reimbursable only for client entertainment, capped at two drinks per
person. Alcohol on solo travel is not reimbursable.

For team meals, the most senior person present pays and submits the expense,
listing every attendee by name.

## Ground transportation

Use rideshare, taxi, or public transit. Rental cars require manager approval and
should be economy or intermediate class. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed
at the current IRS standard rate, which is $0.70 per mile for 2026.

Parking and tolls are reimbursable with a receipt. Traffic and parking fines
are never reimbursable.

## Non-reimbursable

The following are not reimbursed under any circumstance: personal entertainment,
in-room movies, spa and gym charges, childcare, pet boarding, personal travel
extensions, lost baggage not covered by the airline, and expenses for a
travelling companion who is not a Meridian employee.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than four times per year are eligible for a corporate
card. Card transactions still require an expense report with receipts within 30
days. Repeated failure to reconcile within 60 days results in card suspension.

## Approvals

Your manager approves expenses up to $2,500. Director approval is required from
$2,501 to $10,000. Anything above $10,000 requires VP and Finance approval before
the expense is incurred, not after.
