Title: Remote and Hybrid Work Policy
Category: Workplace
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-02-15

## Eligibility

Most roles at Meridian Systems are eligible for hybrid work. Roles requiring
physical presence — facilities, hardware lab, and front desk — are designated
on-site and are marked as such in the job description.

Eligibility is determined by role, not by individual negotiation. If you believe
your role is misclassified, raise it with your manager and People Operations.

## Default schedule

Hybrid employees are expected in their assigned office three days per week.
Tuesday and Wednesday are company-wide anchor days; the third day is chosen by
your team and published in the team calendar.

Teams may adjust anchor days with director approval, but must keep at least two
overlapping in-office days so cross-team collaboration remains possible.

## Fully remote arrangements

Fully remote arrangements require VP approval and are reviewed annually. They
are approved for one of three reasons: the role has no on-site component, the
candidate market for the role is geographically constrained, or an accommodation
is in place.

Fully remote employees are expected to travel to their hub office twice per year
for team planning. Travel is booked and paid by the company under the Travel and
Expense Policy.

## Working from a different location

You may work from a location other than your registered home or office for up to
20 business days per calendar year with manager notification. No approval is
needed.

Working from outside your country of employment requires People Operations
approval regardless of duration, because it creates tax and employment law
obligations for the company. Submit these requests at least 30 days ahead.

## Equipment and workspace

Meridian provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset to all
employees. Remote and hybrid employees may claim up to $500 once every three
years toward a desk, chair, or lighting through the Expense system under the
category "Home Office."

Internet service is not reimbursed. Employees are responsible for a connection
adequate for video calls.

## Core hours and availability

Meridian operates on core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in your registered time zone.
Meetings should be scheduled inside that window. Outside core hours you are not
expected to be responsive.

If your team spans more than six time zones, the team lead should reduce
synchronous meetings rather than expand core hours for anyone.

## Changing your registered location

Notify People Operations at least 30 days before moving. Compensation is tied to
geographic pay zone, so a move may change your salary band. People Operations
will confirm any adjustment in writing before the move takes effect.
