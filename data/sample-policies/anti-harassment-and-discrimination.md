Title: Anti-Harassment and Discrimination Policy
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: Legal
Effective: 2026-01-01

## What this policy covers

This policy prohibits harassment and discrimination based on any
characteristic protected under the Equal Employment Opportunity Policy. It
applies to employees, contractors, interns, and vendors, in the office, while
remote, at company events, and in work-related communication such as Slack
and email.

## Definitions

**Harassment** is unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic that
is severe or pervasive enough to create a hostile work environment, or that
is tied to a decision about someone's employment.

**Sexual harassment** includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for
sexual favors, and other verbal, visual, or physical conduct of a sexual
nature. It includes quid pro quo conduct, where submission to or rejection
of the conduct affects someone's job.

A single severe incident, such as a physical assault or an explicit threat,
can constitute harassment on its own; it does not need to repeat.

## Examples of prohibited conduct

- Slurs, jokes, or comments about a protected characteristic.
- Unwelcome physical contact or invasion of personal space.
- Displaying or sharing sexual or offensive images, including in Slack or on
  a shared screen.
- Repeated unwanted requests for a date after being told no.
- Mocking someone's accent, disability, or religious practice.

Conduct does not need to be directed at the person who reports it. Someone
who witnesses harassment of a colleague may also report it.

## How to report

Report to your manager, any manager you trust, People Operations, Legal, or
the anonymous ethics line at meridian.ethicspoint.com. You do not have to
report to your direct manager, and reporting to HR directly is always an
option, including when the manager is the subject of the report.

## Investigation process

People Operations or Legal opens an investigation within 3 business days of
a report. Both parties are interviewed, along with relevant witnesses.
Investigations typically conclude within 30 days; complex cases are
communicated on status every two weeks.

Confidentiality is maintained to the extent possible while still allowing a
thorough investigation. Details are shared only with people who need to know
to investigate or remediate.

## Outcomes

Substantiated findings result in consequences ranging from a documented
warning to termination, proportional to severity and history. Meridian does
not require a criminal conviction or a specific number of incidents before
acting.

## Retaliation

Retaliation against someone for reporting in good faith, participating in an
investigation, or refusing an unwelcome advance is prohibited and treated as
seriously as the underlying conduct, whether or not the original report is
substantiated.

## False reports

A report made in good faith that turns out to be unsubstantiated is not
punished. A report made with knowledge that it is false is a separate policy
violation.
