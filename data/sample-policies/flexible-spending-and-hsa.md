Title: Flexible Spending and Health Savings Account Policy
Category: Benefits
Owner: Benefits Team
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Available accounts

Which account you can use depends on your health plan election under the
Health Insurance and Benefits Enrollment policy:

- **Health Savings Account (HSA):** available if you enroll in the
  high-deductible health plan. Meridian contributes $750 per year for
  individual coverage or $1,500 for family coverage, deposited in two
  installments in January and July.
- **Health Care Flexible Spending Account (FSA):** available if you
  enroll in the PPO plan. Not available alongside an HSA.
- **Dependent Care FSA:** available regardless of which health plan you
  choose, for eligible child or elder care expenses.
- **Limited Purpose FSA:** available alongside an HSA, for dental and
  vision expenses only.

## Contribution limits

2026 IRS limits apply: $4,300 for individual HSA coverage, $8,550 for
family HSA coverage, $3,300 for a Health Care or Limited Purpose FSA, and
$5,000 for a Dependent Care FSA ($2,500 if married filing separately).
Meridian's HSA contribution counts toward your individual limit.

## Enrollment and changes

Elect your contribution amount during open enrollment each November for
the following plan year, or within 30 days of a qualifying life event such
as marriage, birth, or a change in employment status. Outside of those
windows, your election is locked for the plan year; this is an IRS
requirement, not a Meridian rule.

## HSA ownership and portability

Your HSA is your own account, held with the plan's administrator, and
travels with you if you leave Meridian, including Meridian's contributions
already deposited. There is no "use it or lose it" rule; unused HSA funds
roll over indefinitely and can be invested once your balance exceeds the
administrator's minimum threshold.

## FSA use-it-or-lose-it and carryover

Health Care FSA funds carry over up to $660 into the following year;
anything above that is forfeited if not used by December 31. The Dependent
Care FSA has no carryover; unused funds are forfeited at year end, so
estimate your annual child or elder care costs carefully when electing.

## Using your funds

Both plans provide a debit card for eligible expenses at point of sale.
Keep receipts for every purchase; the administrator periodically requests
documentation to verify an expense was eligible, and undocumented expenses
may be treated as taxable income.

## Questions and claims

Contact the plan administrator directly for claims and card issues; contact
People Operations for enrollment or contribution changes. Details and
current-year limits are on the benefits portal under "FSA/HSA."
