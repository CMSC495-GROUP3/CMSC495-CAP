Title: New Hire Onboarding Guide
Category: Onboarding
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Before your first day

You will receive an offer packet through DocuSign. Complete and return all
documents at least three business days before your start date, including the
signed offer letter, I-9 identity verification, W-4 withholding election, and
direct deposit setup.

Your laptop ships to your home address and typically arrives two business days
before your start date. If it has not arrived the day before you start, email
the IT Service Desk.

## Day one

Orientation runs from 09:00 to 12:00 in your local time zone and is delivered
over Zoom for all locations. The invitation comes from People Operations the week
before you start.

In the afternoon, complete account setup:

1. Set your Okta password and enroll two factors — an authenticator app is
   required, SMS alone is not accepted.
2. Sign in to Slack, Google Workspace, and Workday through Okta.
3. Complete the security awareness training assigned in Workday.
4. Confirm your emergency contact and mailing address in Workday.

Your manager will schedule a first one-on-one within the first two days.

## First week

- Meet your onboarding buddy, assigned by your manager before you start. Your
  buddy is a peer, not your manager, and is there for questions you would rather
  not ask up the chain.
- Complete required compliance training: security awareness, anti-harassment,
  and code of conduct. All three are due within 14 days.
- Enroll in benefits. You have 30 days from your start date, and coverage is
  retroactive to day one. See the Health Insurance and Benefits Enrollment
  policy.
- Set your 401(k) contribution rate. Automatic enrollment at 4% begins after 30
  days if you take no action.

## First 30 days

Your manager will agree a set of 30-day goals with you in week one. These are
about ramp-up and orientation, not delivery — the intent is that you know how
the team works, who does what, and how to ship a small change end to end.

## First 90 days

At 90 days, you and your manager complete a check-in in Workday covering what is
going well, where you are still ramping, and what support you need. This is not
a performance rating and does not feed into the review cycle.

Your first formal performance review happens in the next regular cycle after
you complete six months of service.

## Payroll

Meridian pays semi-monthly on the 15th and the last business day of the month.
If your start date falls mid-period, your first paycheck covers only the days
worked in that period.

## Who to ask

- **IT Service Desk** — hardware, accounts, access, VPN.
- **People Operations** — pay, leave, policy, employment verification.
- **Benefits Team** — enrollment, claims, dependents, HSA and FSA.
- **Your buddy** — anything you would rather ask a peer first.
