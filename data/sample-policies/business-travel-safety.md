Title: Business Travel Safety Policy
Category: Workplace
Owner: Facilities
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Scope

This policy covers safety and duty-of-care requirements for company
business travel. See the Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy for
booking process and expense rules.

## Before you travel

Book travel through the approved travel platform (Navan) so Meridian has a
record of your itinerary; this is what allows Meridian to reach you in an
emergency. Register international trips at least 10 business days in
advance so Facilities can check the destination's current travel advisory
level.

Trips to a country under a U.S. State Department Level 3 or 4 advisory
require VP approval in advance and a pre-travel briefing from Facilities.

## While traveling

Keep your phone reachable and check in with your manager if your itinerary
changes materially, such as an extended stay or an added destination.
Meridian's travel platform includes a 24/7 emergency assistance line,
listed on your itinerary confirmation, for medical emergencies, lost
documents, or civil unrest at your destination.

## Emergency evacuation and assistance

Meridian's travel insurance, included automatically on trips booked through
the approved platform, covers emergency medical evacuation and repatriation.
If a natural disaster, civil unrest, or another emergency affects your
destination, Facilities and your manager will contact you using the
itinerary information; follow instructions from local authorities first,
then Meridian's guidance.

## Solo travel and high-risk activity

Solo travel is permitted for standard business trips. Extended solo travel
to a remote or high-risk location requires a manager-approved check-in
schedule, such as a daily message. Recreational high-risk activity (for
example, technical climbing or scuba diving) during a business trip is not
covered by Meridian's travel insurance and is undertaken at your own risk.

## Ground transportation

Use rideshare, taxi, or hotel-arranged transportation rather than an
unlicensed vehicle. Renting a car is permitted through the approved travel
platform; personal vehicle use for business travel beyond your normal
commute is reimbursed at the standard mileage rate under the Travel and
Expense Reimbursement Policy but is not covered by Meridian's travel
insurance.

## Incident reporting

Report any safety incident, theft, medical issue, or near-miss during
business travel to Facilities and your manager within 24 hours, using the
same incident form referenced in the Workplace Health and Safety Policy.
