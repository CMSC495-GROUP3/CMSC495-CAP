Title: Payroll and Timekeeping
Category: Finance
Owner: Payroll
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Pay schedule

Meridian Systems pays semi-monthly. Paydays are the 15th and the last business
day of each month. When a payday falls on a weekend or company holiday, payment
is issued on the preceding business day.

There are 24 pay periods per year. Benefit premiums, 401(k) contributions, and
other deductions are spread evenly across all 24.

## Pay statements

Statements are available in Workday on payday morning. They are retained for
seven years and remain accessible after you leave the company for as long as your
Workday account is active, which is 90 days post-separation. Download anything
you need before then.

## Direct deposit

Direct deposit is required. You may split pay across up to three accounts.
Changes made before the 5th take effect on the 15th; changes made after the 5th
take effect the following pay period, because banking pre-notes take several days
to verify.

## Timekeeping for non-exempt employees

Non-exempt (hourly) employees record time daily in Workday. Timesheets are due by
10:00 on the first business day after the period closes. Managers approve by
14:00 the same day.

Record actual hours worked, including any work performed outside scheduled hours.
Do not record meal breaks of 30 minutes or more as worked time.

Failing to submit a timesheet on time delays payment to the following period.
Meridian cannot issue off-cycle payments for late timesheets except where
required by state law.

## Overtime

Non-exempt employees receive 1.5x their regular rate for hours worked beyond 40
in a workweek. Some states require daily overtime; where state law is more
generous, state law applies.

Overtime requires advance manager approval. Working unapproved overtime is a
policy issue, not a pay issue — the hours are still paid.

Exempt (salaried) employees do not receive overtime.

## Corrections

If your pay is wrong, contact Payroll within 5 business days. Underpayments of
more than $200 are corrected by off-cycle payment within 3 business days.
Smaller underpayments are corrected on the next regular payday.

Overpayments are recovered through a repayment schedule agreed in writing. Meridian
will not deduct more than 15% of net pay per period without your consent.

## Withholding

Update your W-4 in Workday at any time. Changes apply to the next pay period
that has not yet been processed. Payroll cannot advise you on how to complete a
W-4 — consult a tax professional.

## Final pay

Final paychecks are issued according to state law, which in some states requires
payment on the last day of work and in others allows payment on the next regular
payday. Payroll confirms the date during offboarding. Accrued PTO payout follows
the Paid Time Off policy.

## Employment and income verification

Direct verification requests to People Operations. Meridian confirms dates of
employment and job title to any requester, and confirms salary only with your
written authorization.
