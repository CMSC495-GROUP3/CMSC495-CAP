Title: Bereavement Leave Policy
Category: Time Off & Leave
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Who is eligible

All regular full-time and part-time employees are eligible from their first
day of employment. There is no waiting period.

## How much leave

- **Immediate family** (spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, sibling,
  grandparent, or grandchild): 5 paid days.
- **Extended family** (aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, cousin, or in-law): 3
  paid days.
- **Miscarriage or stillbirth:** 5 paid days, available to either parent,
  separate from and in addition to any short-term disability leave.
- **Close friend or other significant loss** not covered above: 2 paid days,
  at manager discretion.

Days do not need to be consecutive and may be split to accommodate a
funeral, a memorial service, and related travel, as long as they are used
within 60 days of the loss.

## Requesting leave

Notify your manager as soon as practical; a specific reason does not need
to be provided in advance if you're not ready to share it. Enter the leave
in Workday under "Bereavement" when you're able to; People Operations will
backdate the entry if needed.

## Extending beyond the paid allotment

Additional unpaid leave, or use of PTO, is available on request for
circumstances requiring more time, such as settling an estate or travel to
another country. Talk to your manager or People Operations; these requests
are approved except where there is a genuine, documented coverage problem.

## Pay and benefits

Bereavement leave is paid at 100% of base pay and does not draw down your
PTO balance. Benefits continue without interruption.

## Support resources

Meridian's Employee Assistance Program, available through the health
benefits provider, offers free grief counseling sessions. Contact
information is on the benefits portal under "EAP."
