Title: Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Policy
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: Information Security
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Scope

This policy covers personal phones, tablets, and computers used to access
Meridian systems. It supplements the IT Acceptable Use and Security Policy,
which governs company-owned devices.

## What's allowed on a personal device

Email, calendar, and Slack are approved for personal device access through
the mobile apps, protected by mobile device management (MDM) enrollment.
Personal computers may access Google Workspace through the browser without
MDM enrollment, since browser access does not download company data to
local storage.

Company source code, customer data, and confidential documents may not be
downloaded to or stored on a personal device under any circumstance. Use a
company-managed laptop for any work involving that data; see IT if your
role requires this access and you don't yet have a company laptop.

## Enrollment requirements

Enrolling a personal phone for email and Slack access requires installing
Meridian's MDM profile, which enforces a passcode or biometric lock,
encryption, and the ability to remotely wipe the Meridian-managed portion
of the device (referred to as a "container wipe") without touching your
personal data, photos, or apps.

MDM enrollment is done through the Self Service portal and takes about 10
minutes. IT support is available for enrollment issues.

## Lost or stolen personal devices

Report a lost or stolen personal device enrolled in MDM to the IT Service
Desk immediately, at any hour, the same as a company device under the IT
Acceptable Use and Security Policy. IT initiates a container wipe, removing
only Meridian's managed apps and data, within one hour of a report.

## Departure

Meridian data on a personal device is removed automatically as part of
offboarding, through a container wipe that does not affect personal data.
You're expected to complete this removal, or allow IT to trigger it
remotely, on your last day.

## What Meridian does not access

Meridian's MDM tool does not have visibility into personal photos,
messages, personal email, or apps outside the managed work container. IT
cannot see your personal browsing or location through this enrollment.

## Opting out

If you prefer not to enroll a personal device, you can request a
company-owned phone or continue using company hardware exclusively for
Meridian systems; there's no requirement to use a personal device for work.
