Title: Introductory Period Policy
Category: Onboarding
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

Meridian's first 90 days of employment are an introductory period, giving
new employees and their managers a structured window to confirm the role
is a good fit on both sides. Employment remains at-will throughout and
after the introductory period; see the Disciplinary Action and Termination
Policy for how at-will employment works.

## What's different during the introductory period

- **Benefits eligibility.** Health benefits begin on your first day
  regardless of the introductory period; see the Health Insurance and
  Benefits Enrollment policy. Tuition reimbursement and the employee
  referral bonus require 6 months of service, extending past this window.
- **PTO usage.** PTO accrues from day one and may be used immediately;
  there is no separate waiting period tied to the introductory period.
- **Check-ins.** Your manager schedules structured check-ins at 30, 60, and
  90 days, covering role clarity, early performance signals, and any
  support you need. These are separate from the annual performance review
  cycle.

## Extending the introductory period

A manager may extend the introductory period once, by up to 60 days, when
more time is needed to evaluate fit, most often due to a slow ramp-up or a
role with a long learning curve. An extension is communicated to the
employee in writing with specific areas of focus, and requires People
Operations sign-off.

## Ending employment during the introductory period

Either the employee or Meridian may end the employment relationship at any
time during the introductory period, consistent with Meridian's general
at-will policy. A separation during this period does not require the
progressive discipline steps described in the Disciplinary Action and
Termination Policy, though People Operations sign-off is still required
before an involuntary separation.

## Completing the introductory period

There is no separate action required to "complete" the introductory
period; if employment continues past 90 days (or an approved extension),
the employee moves to standard employment status automatically, and the
90-day check-in structure is replaced by regular 1:1s and the standard
performance review cycle.
