Title: Equal Employment Opportunity Policy
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: Legal
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Policy statement

Meridian Systems hires, promotes, trains, and pays employees based on
qualifications and performance. Employment decisions do not consider race,
color, religion, sex, pregnancy, national origin, age, disability, genetic
information, veteran status, sexual orientation, gender identity, marital
status, or any other characteristic protected by law.

This policy applies to every stage of employment: recruiting, hiring,
compensation, benefits, promotion, discipline, and termination.

## Recruiting and hiring

Job postings describe the role and its requirements, not a preferred
candidate profile. Interview panels use a shared scorecard tied to the
role's requirements so that candidates are compared on the same criteria.

Recruiters and hiring managers complete unconscious bias training before
running their first interview loop and annually thereafter.

## Compensation equity

People Operations runs a pay equity analysis every year, comparing
compensation across protected groups for employees in the same role and
level. Unexplained gaps above 3% are corrected in the next compensation
cycle regardless of individual performance ratings.

Managers do not have discretion to set starting pay outside the published
band for a role and level. See the Payroll and Timekeeping policy for band
structure.

## Reasonable accommodation

Meridian provides reasonable accommodation for disability, pregnancy, and
religious practice. See the Disability and Workplace Accommodation Policy
for the request process.

## Reporting a concern

Report a concern about discrimination to your manager, People Operations,
Legal, or the anonymous ethics line at meridian.ethicspoint.com. Reports are
investigated by People Operations or Legal, not by the manager involved.

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited
and is itself grounds for discipline, independent of whether the underlying
concern is substantiated.

## Government reporting

Meridian files an annual EEO-1 report and retains hiring and personnel
records for the period required by federal and state law. Employees are not
required to disclose protected characteristics; voluntary self-identification
during the application process is used only in aggregate for compliance
reporting and is not visible to hiring managers.
