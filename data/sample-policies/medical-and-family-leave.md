Title: Medical and Family Leave Policy
Category: Time Off & Leave
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This fictional Meridian Technologies policy describes employer-provided medical
and family leave for eligible employees. It is not a statement of universal
legal entitlement. Statutory leave rights vary by jurisdiction; when law and
this policy both apply, Meridian follows the provision more favorable to the
employee after People Operations confirms eligibility.

## Who may request leave

Regular full-time employees who have completed 12 months of service and worked
at least 1,250 hours in the prior 12 months may request up to 12 weeks of leave
in a rolling 12-month period for:

- the employee's own serious health condition;
- bonding with a newborn, newly adopted child, or newly placed foster child;
- caring for a spouse, domestic partner, child, or parent with a serious health
  condition.

Part-time and newly hired employees should contact People Operations to learn
what, if any, analogous leave Meridian can offer in their situation.

## Pay and job protection during leave

Medical and family leave under this policy is unpaid unless the employee elects
to use accrued PTO or short-term disability (when separately eligible). Meridian
continues active medical, dental, and vision coverage on the same premium share
during an approved leave of 12 weeks or less. Employees remain responsible for
their portion of premiums; missed premiums are collected on return or through
COBRA if coverage ends.

## How to request leave

Submit a leave request in Workday at least 30 days in advance when the need is
foreseeable, or as soon as practical otherwise. People Operations may request
medical certification for serious health conditions. Do not interpret eligibility,
duration, or intermittent scheduling on your own—confirm with People Operations
before making coverage commitments.

## Related policies

See the Sick and Safe Leave Policy for short-term illness and safety absences,
and the Benefits Continuation (COBRA) Policy if coverage would otherwise end.
