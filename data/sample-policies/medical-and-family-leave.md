Title: Medical and Family Leave Policy
Category: Time Off & Leave
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This fictional Meridian Systems policy describes employer-provided medical
and family leave for eligible employees. Company medical and family leave under
this policy is distinct from statutory FMLA leave. When FMLA (or another leave
statute) applies to an employee, People Operations administers statutory rights
separately from this company benefit; when both apply to the same absence,
Meridian follows the provision more favorable to the employee after confirming
eligibility. This document is not a statement of universal legal entitlement.

## Who may request company medical and family leave

Regular full-time employees who have completed 12 months of service and worked
at least 1,250 hours in the prior 12 months may request up to 12 weeks of
company medical and family leave in a rolling 12-month period for:

- the employee's own serious health condition;
- caring for a spouse, domestic partner, child, or parent with a serious health
  condition.

Bonding with a newborn, newly adopted child, or newly placed foster child is
governed by the Parental Leave Policy. This Medical and Family Leave Policy does
not add a second company bonding-leave entitlement or stack additional Meridian
bonding pay or weeks on top of Parental Leave. When People Operations determines
that FMLA or a state family-leave program applies to the same bonding absence,
those statutory rights may run concurrently with Parental Leave rather than as
extra company bonding weeks.

Part-time and newly hired employees should contact People Operations to learn
what, if any, analogous company leave Meridian can offer in their situation.
Meeting these company eligibility markers does not by itself establish FMLA
eligibility; FMLA coverage depends on statutory employer and employee tests that
People Operations evaluates separately.

## Pay and job protection during leave

Company medical and family leave under this policy is unpaid unless the employee
elects to use accrued PTO or short-term disability (when separately eligible).
Meridian continues active medical, dental, and vision coverage on the same
premium share during an approved company leave of 12 weeks or less. Employees
remain responsible for their portion of premiums; missed premiums are collected
on return or through COBRA if coverage ends.

## How to request leave

Submit a leave request in Workday at least 30 days in advance when the need is
foreseeable, or as soon as practical otherwise. People Operations may request
medical certification for serious health conditions and will say whether the
absence is designated as company leave, FMLA, both, or another statutory leave.
Do not interpret eligibility, duration, or intermittent scheduling on your
own—confirm with People Operations before making coverage commitments.
