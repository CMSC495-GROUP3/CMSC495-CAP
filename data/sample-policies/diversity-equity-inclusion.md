Title: Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Policy
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This policy describes Meridian's ongoing programs to build a workforce and
culture where people of different backgrounds succeed on equal terms. It
supplements the Equal Employment Opportunity Policy, which sets the
non-discrimination baseline; this policy covers the additional programs
Meridian runs beyond that baseline.

## Hiring practices

Open roles are posted for a minimum of 10 business days and shared with at
least two organizations serving underrepresented groups in tech, in
addition to standard job boards. Interview panels include at least one
person from outside the hiring team where team size allows.

Recruiting tracks candidate pipeline diversity in aggregate at the funnel
stage, not the individual level, to identify where qualified candidates are
dropping out of the process.

## Employee resource groups

Meridian supports employee resource groups (ERGs), each with an executive
sponsor and a small annual budget for events and speakers. Current ERGs are
listed on the intranet under "Community"; any group of 5 or more employees
may propose a new ERG to People Operations.

ERG participation is voluntary and unpaid, though meetings during work
hours up to one hour per month are treated as work time, not personal time.

## Manager training

Managers complete inclusive leadership training within 90 days of becoming
a people manager and a refresher every two years. Training covers
interviewing without bias, giving equitable feedback, and running inclusive
meetings.

## Pay and promotion equity

See the Equal Employment Opportunity Policy for the annual pay equity
analysis. Promotion rates by demographic group are reviewed annually by
People Operations leadership; a material gap triggers a review of the
promotion criteria and calibration process for the following cycle.

## Accessibility

Meridian's offices, products, and internal tools are built to meet WCAG 2.1
AA accessibility standards where practicable. Report an accessibility
barrier, in the office or in an internal tool, to Facilities or IT and it
will be logged and prioritized against a 90-day remediation target.

## Measuring progress

People Operations publishes an internal annual diversity report each
January, summarizing workforce composition, hiring, and retention trends in
aggregate. Individual data is never shared at a level that could identify a
specific employee.
