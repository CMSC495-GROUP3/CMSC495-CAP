Title: Overtime and Compensation Structure Policy
Category: Finance
Owner: Payroll
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Exempt and non-exempt classification

Every role at Meridian is classified as exempt or non-exempt under the Fair
Labor Standards Act (FLSA) and applicable state law. Your offer letter and
Workday profile state your classification. Classification is based on job
duties and salary level, not job title, and is reviewed by Payroll whenever
a role changes materially.

## Non-exempt employees and overtime

Non-exempt employees are paid 1.5 times their regular hourly rate for hours
worked beyond 40 in a workweek. Some states require daily overtime beyond 8
hours in a day or double time beyond 12 hours; Payroll applies the rule
that is more favorable to the employee based on work state.

Overtime must be approved by your manager in advance where practical.
Unapproved overtime that is actually worked is still paid in full; working
it without approval is a conversation with your manager about planning, not
a reason to withhold pay.

Time worked, not time scheduled, determines overtime. See the Payroll and
Timekeeping policy for how to record hours accurately.

## Exempt employees

Exempt employees are paid a fixed salary that does not vary based on hours
worked in a given week, and are not eligible for overtime pay. Occasional
work outside normal hours is expected as part of many exempt roles, but
should not become a routine expectation without a conversation with your
manager about workload.

## Salary bands and structure

Every role and level maps to a published salary band, reviewed annually by
People Operations and Finance against market data. Managers do not have
discretion to offer or pay outside the band for a role and level without a
documented exception approved by a VP and People Operations.

## Merit increases and market adjustments

Merit increases are awarded during the annual compensation cycle each
March, tied to performance rating; see the Performance Reviews and
Promotion policy. Off-cycle market adjustments may be made at any time when
Finance identifies a role has fallen materially behind current market data,
independent of individual performance.

## Bonuses

Eligible roles participate in an annual bonus plan with a target percentage
of base salary set by level, funded based on company and individual
performance. Bonus payouts are made by March 15 following the performance
year. Bonus eligibility and target percentages are documented in your offer
letter.

## Pay transparency

Salary ranges are posted on every internal and external job listing.
Employees may discuss their own pay with anyone; retaliation for discussing
compensation is prohibited under this policy and under the National Labor
Relations Act.
