Title: Whistleblower Policy
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: Legal
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This policy protects employees who report a suspected violation of law,
regulation, or Meridian policy, including financial misconduct, safety
violations, and fraud. It is separate from the Anti-Harassment and
Discrimination Policy, which covers harassment reports specifically.

## What to report

Report a good-faith concern about:

- Financial fraud, accounting irregularities, or securities law violations.
- Bribery or corruption, including violations of the Anti-Bribery and Gifts
  Policy.
- Safety violations that create a risk of injury.
- Illegal discrimination or harassment.
- Falsification of company records, including expense reports, timesheets,
  and customer contracts.
- Retaliation against another whistleblower.

You do not need proof, only a good-faith, reasonable belief that a
violation occurred or is occurring.

## How to report

Report through any of these channels:

- Your manager or any other manager.
- Legal, directly or through legal@meridian.io.
- The anonymous ethics line at meridian.ethicspoint.com, operated by an
  independent third party and available by phone or web, 24 hours a day.
- The Audit Committee of the Board, for concerns involving executive
  leadership, at auditcommittee@meridian.io.

Reports to the ethics line can be made anonymously. Meridian does not
attempt to unmask an anonymous reporter.

## Investigation

Legal reviews every report within 5 business days and assigns it to the
appropriate investigator, who may be internal or an outside firm for
sensitive matters. Investigations are documented and concluded with a
written finding. The reporter, if not anonymous, receives a summary of the
outcome to the extent confidentiality allows.

## Protection against retaliation

Retaliation against a good-faith whistleblower is strictly prohibited,
including termination, demotion, reduced hours, exclusion from meetings, or
a hostile shift in how someone is treated. This protection applies whether
or not the underlying concern is ultimately substantiated, as long as the
report was made in good faith.

An employee who believes they have experienced retaliation for a report may
raise it through any of the channels above; retaliation complaints are
prioritized and reviewed within 5 business days.

## Knowingly false reports

Good-faith reports that turn out to be mistaken are protected. A report made
with knowledge that it is false, intended to harm a colleague, is itself a
policy violation and addressed under the Disciplinary Action and Termination
Policy.

## Legal protections

Nothing in this policy limits your right to report a suspected violation of
law directly to a government agency, including the SEC, OSHA, or the EEOC,
or to receive a whistleblower award where a law provides one. You do not
need to report internally first.
