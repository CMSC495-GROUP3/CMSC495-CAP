Title: Relocation Assistance Policy
Category: Finance
Owner: Finance
Effective: 2026-01-01

## When relocation assistance applies

Relocation assistance is available for a new hire or current employee
moving more than 50 miles to work from a Meridian office, when the move is
initiated by Meridian, such as a role that requires on-site presence at a
specific location. It is not available for a voluntary move to a location
convenient for personal reasons.

## Assistance tiers

| Situation | Assistance |
|-----------|------------|
| New hire, individual contributor | Up to $7,500, lump sum |
| New hire, manager and above | Up to $15,000, lump sum |
| Current employee, company-initiated transfer | Full moving costs covered directly, plus $3,000 lump sum for incidentals |
| International relocation | Case by case, coordinated with Legal and an immigration provider |

Lump sum amounts are intended to cover moving costs, temporary housing, and
incidentals at the employee's discretion, and are paid within the first
payroll cycle after your start date.

## What's covered for company-initiated transfers

For a full-coverage transfer, Meridian directly pays a moving company for
household goods, up to 30 days of temporary housing, and a one-time house
hunting trip for you and a spouse or partner (up to 3 days). Submit receipts
through the Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy process for anything
paid out of pocket and eligible for reimbursement.

## Tax treatment

Relocation lump sums and reimbursements are taxable income under current
federal law and are subject to standard payroll withholding. Meridian does
not gross up relocation payments to offset the tax impact.

## Repayment on early departure

If you resign within 12 months of a Meridian-funded relocation, a prorated
portion of the relocation assistance is deducted from your final paycheck,
to the extent permitted by state law: the full amount if you resign within
3 months, decreasing on a straight-line basis to zero at 12 months. This
does not apply if Meridian initiates the termination.

## Requesting relocation assistance

Discuss relocation with your hiring manager (for a new hire) or your
current manager and Finance (for a transfer) before the move. Finance
issues a relocation agreement outlining the assistance amount and repayment
terms, which must be signed before funds are disbursed.
