Title: Employee Data Privacy Policy
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: Information Security
Effective: 2026-01-01

## What data Meridian collects

Meridian collects employee data needed to run payroll and benefits, manage
performance, comply with employment law, and secure company systems. This
includes contact and demographic information, compensation history,
performance reviews, IT system logs, and badge access records. Data
collected during recruiting, such as interview notes, is also employee data
once you're hired and is retained under the same rules.

## How employee data is used

Data is used for the purpose it was collected for: payroll data for pay and
tax reporting, performance data for review cycles, badge data for building
security and safety. Data is not used for a new purpose without a
documented business reason reviewed by Legal and People Operations.

## Who can access your data

- **People Operations** has broad access to personnel records to administer
  employment.
- **Your manager** sees performance, compensation, and attendance data for
  people who report to them, directly or indirectly.
- **IT and Information Security** access system logs for security
  monitoring and troubleshooting, not for evaluating performance.
- **Payroll and Benefits vendors** receive the specific data needed to
  process pay and administer benefits, under a data processing agreement
  that limits their use of it.

Access is logged. People Operations periodically audits access to
sensitive records such as medical or accommodation files.

## Monitoring

Meridian monitors network traffic, email sent through company systems, and
device security posture for security and compliance purposes, as described
in the IT Acceptable Use and Security Policy. Meridian does not read the
content of personal messaging apps or personal email, even on a company
device, and does not monitor keystrokes or take periodic screenshots.

Badge access logs are retained for building security purposes and reviewed
only in connection with a specific security incident or investigation, not
for routine attendance tracking.

## Retention

Core employment records are retained for 7 years after separation, in line
with federal and state recordkeeping requirements. Recruiting data for
candidates not hired is retained for 2 years. Security logs are retained
for 1 year unless relevant to an open investigation.

## Your rights

You may request a copy of your own personnel file from People Operations;
requests are fulfilled within 15 business days. You may request correction
of inaccurate data. Where applicable state law (such as the California
Consumer Privacy Act) provides additional rights, Meridian honors them
regardless of which state you work in.

## Data sharing outside Meridian

Employee data is shared outside Meridian only for a legitimate business
purpose: payroll and benefits administration, legally required reporting,
or a background check you've consented to. Meridian does not sell employee
data.

## Data breach notification

If a breach affecting employee data occurs, Information Security notifies
affected employees within the timeframe required by applicable law,
generally within 30 days of confirming the breach, along with a description
of what data was involved and what steps Meridian is taking.
