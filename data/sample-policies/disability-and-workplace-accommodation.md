Title: Disability and Workplace Accommodation Policy
Category: Benefits
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Policy statement

Meridian provides reasonable accommodation to qualified employees with a
disability, and to employees whose religious practice or pregnancy requires
a workplace adjustment, unless the accommodation would create an undue
hardship on the business.

## Requesting an accommodation

Start a request with People Operations, your manager, or by emailing
accommodations@meridian.io. You do not need to use the word "accommodation"
or disclose a diagnosis; describing the workplace barrier is enough to start
the process.

People Operations acknowledges a request within 3 business days and begins
an interactive dialogue with you to identify options. A request generally
does not require a doctor's note for straightforward accommodations, such as
a keyboard tray or a schedule shift; documentation may be requested for more
significant changes, such as extended leave.

## Common accommodations

- Ergonomic equipment: adjustable desk, chair, monitor arm, keyboard.
- Modified schedule or flexible start time.
- Remote work for a role normally performed on-site.
- Modified break schedule for medical needs.
- Assistive technology: screen readers, speech-to-text software.
- Reserved parking or accessible workspace location.

Most equipment-based accommodations are approved and fulfilled within 10
business days. More complex requests, such as a schedule or role
modification, are typically resolved within 30 days.

## Confidentiality

Medical information related to an accommodation request is kept in a file
separate from your general personnel file, accessible only to People
Operations and anyone with a direct need to implement the accommodation.
Your manager is told what adjustment is being made, not the underlying
medical reason, unless you choose to share it.

## Leave as an accommodation

Extended leave can itself be a reasonable accommodation when other options
are not workable. This is evaluated case by case and coordinated with any
applicable short-term disability benefit or FMLA leave; contact People
Operations before the leave begins so it is classified correctly.

## Undue hardship

Meridian may decline a specific requested accommodation if it would impose
a significant difficulty or expense, but will work with you to identify an
alternative that meets the underlying need. A denial is documented in
writing with the reason and reviewed by People Operations leadership before
being finalized.

## Retaliation

Requesting or using an accommodation has no effect on performance reviews,
promotion eligibility, or job security. Retaliation for requesting an
accommodation is prohibited and handled under the Disciplinary Action and
Termination Policy.
