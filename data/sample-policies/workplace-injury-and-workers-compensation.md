Title: Workplace Injury and Workers' Compensation Policy
Category: Health & Safety
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This fictional Meridian Technologies policy explains how employees report
workplace injuries and how Meridian coordinates workers' compensation claims.
Workers' compensation coverage and benefit levels are set by applicable law and
the insurance carrier; this policy does not promise a specific benefit amount.

## Immediate steps after an injury

1. Get medical attention for any serious injury.
2. Notify your manager or People Operations as soon as practical, and no later
   than the end of the shift when possible.
3. Complete an incident report in Workday under Workplace Injury.

Failure to report promptly can delay claim handling; it does not by itself forfeit
legal rights that a jurisdiction may preserve.

## Claim handling

People Operations files the employer notice with Meridian's workers' compensation
carrier. The carrier, not your manager, decides claim acceptance and wage-replacement
amounts. Employees should direct benefit questions to the carrier adjuster copied
on the claim acknowledgment, and escalate process problems to People Operations.

## Return to work

Meridian offers transitional duty when the treating provider releases the employee
to modified work and a suitable assignment exists. Do not invent work restrictions
or clear yourself to full duty—follow the provider's written note and People
Operations' return-to-work plan.
