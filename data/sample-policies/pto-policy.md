Title: Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy
Category: Time Off & Leave
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Overview

Meridian Systems provides paid time off to all regular full-time and part-time
employees. PTO covers vacation, personal days, and short-term illness in a
single balance. You do not need to categorize a request as sick versus vacation.
Safe-leave process and jurisdiction questions are covered in the Sick and Safe
Leave Policy; they still draw from this combined PTO bank unless People
Operations directs otherwise for a local ordinance.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue PTO each pay period based on length of service:

| Years of service | Annual accrual | Per pay period (24 periods) |
|------------------|----------------|------------------------------|
| 0 through 2      | 15 days        | 5.00 hours                   |
| 3 through 5      | 20 days        | 6.67 hours                   |
| 6 or more        | 25 days        | 8.33 hours                   |

Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week accrue on a prorated
basis according to scheduled hours.

Accrual begins on your first day of employment. New employees may use accrued
PTO immediately; there is no waiting period.

## Maximum balance and carryover

You may carry a maximum of 10 unused days into the following calendar year. Any
balance above 10 days on December 31 is forfeited. Accrual stops once your
balance reaches 30 days and resumes automatically once you use time and drop
below that ceiling.

We do not pay out unused PTO except at separation, where state law requires it.

## Requesting time off

Submit requests in Workday at least 10 business days in advance for absences of
three days or more, and at least 2 business days in advance for shorter
absences. Your manager will approve or decline within 3 business days.

For unplanned illness, notify your manager as early as possible on the day of
the absence, by whatever channel your team normally uses. Retroactive PTO
requests for sick days should be entered within 3 business days of returning.
Follow the Sick and Safe Leave Policy for notice and documentation steps when
the absence is illness-related or safe leave; the time still comes from this
combined PTO balance.

Requests are generally approved unless coverage is a genuine problem. If a
request is declined, your manager must offer alternative dates.

## Absences of more than five consecutive days

Any absence longer than five consecutive business days requires approval from
both your manager and People Operations. If the absence is medical, it may be
covered under short-term disability, company medical and family leave, or
statutory FMLA rather than PTO — contact People Operations before your leave
begins so it is classified correctly. Company medical leave and FMLA are
distinct; see the Medical and Family Leave Policy. Bonding leave and pay for
new parents are governed by the Parental Leave Policy, not by stacking
additional company medical and family leave weeks.

## Holidays

Company holidays are separate from PTO and are not deducted from your balance.
Meridian observes eleven paid holidays per year, published each November for the
following year. Employees required to work a holiday receive an alternate day
off within 60 days.

## Separation

Accrued and unused PTO is paid out in the final paycheck where required by state
law. In states without a payout requirement, Meridian pays out up to 10 days as
a matter of company policy.
