Title: Record Retention Policy
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: Legal
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This policy sets how long Meridian keeps different categories of business
records, so records are available when needed for legal, tax, and
operational purposes, and disposed of once they no longer need to be kept.

## Retention schedule

| Record type | Retention period |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Personnel files (active employees) | Duration of employment plus 7 years |
| Recruiting records (not hired) | 2 years |
| Payroll and tax records | 7 years |
| I-9 forms | 3 years after hire or 1 year after termination, whichever is later |
| Accommodation and medical records | 7 years, stored separately from the general personnel file |
| Financial statements and general ledger | 7 years |
| Signed customer contracts | 7 years after contract termination |
| Board and committee minutes | Permanent |
| Security incident records | 3 years after resolution, longer if related to active litigation |
| Marketing materials | 3 years |

## Legal holds

When Legal issues a litigation hold notice, normal retention schedules for
the specified records are suspended, and no covered record may be deleted
or altered until Legal lifts the hold, regardless of what this policy or
any automated deletion process would otherwise do. Legal notifies affected
employees directly when a hold applies to records they manage.

## Disposal

Once a retention period expires and no legal hold applies, records are
disposed of securely: physical documents are shredded, and digital records
are deleted, including from backups on their normal rotation schedule.
Employees do not need to manually delete expired records; IT runs an
automated retention and deletion process for most systems, described in
the IT Acceptable Use and Security Policy.

## Employee responsibilities

Store business records in approved company systems (Google Workspace,
Workday, the relevant business application) rather than local files or
personal accounts, so they're covered by the correct retention and legal
hold process. Do not independently delete a record you believe may be
relevant to a legal matter; ask Legal first.

## Questions

Contact Legal for a retention question not covered by the schedule above,
or if you're uncertain whether a legal hold applies to something you're
about to delete.
