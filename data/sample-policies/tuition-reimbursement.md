Title: Tuition Reimbursement and Professional Development Policy
Category: Career
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Who is eligible

Regular full-time employees are eligible after 6 months of service.
Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week are eligible on a
prorated basis after the same 6-month mark.

## Tuition reimbursement

Meridian reimburses up to $5,250 per calendar year for tuition, required
fees, and required textbooks for a degree program, certificate program, or
individual course relevant to your current role or a reasonable career path
at Meridian. Grades of B or better, or "pass" for pass/fail courses, are
required for reimbursement.

Submit the Tuition Reimbursement Request form to your manager and People
Operations before the course begins, along with the course description and
cost. Approval typically takes 5 business days. Reimbursement is paid within
30 days of submitting a final transcript and proof of payment.

The $5,250 annual limit reflects the current IRS threshold for tax-free
employer education assistance; amounts above that limit are still eligible
for reimbursement but are treated as taxable income.

## Conferences and external training

Managers have a discretionary budget for conferences, workshops, and paid
courses (such as certification bootcamps) directly relevant to an
employee's current role. This is separate from the tuition reimbursement
above and does not draw down the $5,250 annual limit. Talk to your manager
about what's available in your team's budget.

## Certifications

Meridian covers the cost of a first attempt at a job-relevant professional
certification exam (for example, a cloud provider certification or a PMP),
requested in advance through your manager. A failed first attempt does not
need to be repaid; a second attempt is covered at manager discretion.

## Repayment on early departure

If you resign within 12 months of completing a reimbursed degree or
certificate program costing more than $2,000, Meridian may deduct a
prorated portion of the reimbursement from your final paycheck, to the
extent permitted by state law. This does not apply to individual courses
under $2,000 or to any termination initiated by Meridian.

## Internal learning resources

Meridian provides free access to an online learning platform (LinkedIn
Learning) for every employee, and internal skills workshops run monthly,
announced in the #learning-and-development Slack channel. These do not
require approval or count against any reimbursement limit.
