Title: Code of Conduct
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: Legal
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Scope

This code applies to every employee, contractor, and intern at Meridian Systems,
in every setting connected to work — offices, remote work, customer sites,
conferences, work travel, and company communication channels.

## Expected behavior

Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Disagree with ideas
rather than people. Assume competence and good intent, and ask before concluding
that someone acted badly.

Be honest in your work. Do not misrepresent results, timelines, credentials, or
the state of a project. Raising a problem early is always preferred to
discovering it late.

## Prohibited conduct

The following are prohibited and may result in discipline up to termination:

- Harassment or discrimination based on race, color, religion, national origin,
  sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, disability, veteran status,
  pregnancy, or any other protected characteristic.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern or participates in an
  investigation.
- Threats, intimidation, or violence of any kind.
- Theft, fraud, or falsification of company records including expense reports
  and timesheets.
- Being impaired by alcohol or drugs while working.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential company, customer, or employee
  information.

## Conflicts of interest

Disclose any outside activity that could reasonably appear to conflict with your
work: outside employment in the same industry, a financial interest in a
competitor, customer, or vendor, or a close personal relationship with someone at
a company we do business with.

Disclosure is not a prohibition. Most conflicts are managed easily once known.
Failing to disclose is the problem.

## Relationships at work

Romantic relationships between employees are not prohibited, but a relationship
within a direct or indirect reporting line must be disclosed to People Operations
so the reporting line can be changed. The more senior person is responsible for
disclosing.

## Gifts and entertainment

You may accept gifts of nominal value, up to $100 per source per year. Never
accept cash or cash equivalents. Never accept anything if it is offered while a
purchasing decision is open.

Anything involving a government official requires Legal approval in advance,
regardless of value.

## Confidential information

Company confidential information includes unreleased product plans, financials,
customer lists, security details, and employee data. Your obligation to protect
it continues after you leave Meridian.

Do not enter confidential information into third-party services that have not
been approved — see the IT Acceptable Use Policy for the approved list.

## Raising a concern

Report concerns to your manager, any other manager you trust, People Operations,
or Legal. An anonymous ethics line is available at meridian.ethicspoint.com and
is operated by an outside provider.

Every report is reviewed. Investigations are kept as confidential as the
circumstances allow. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a
terminable offense, independent of whether the original concern is substantiated.

## Acknowledgment

All employees complete code of conduct training within 14 days of hire and
annually thereafter.
