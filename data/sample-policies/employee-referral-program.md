Title: Employee Referral Program
Category: Career
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Who can refer

Any regular employee may submit a referral for an open role, except
employees on the Recruiting team, who are not eligible for a referral bonus
since sourcing candidates is part of their job.

## How to refer someone

Submit the referral through the careers portal at meridian.io/careers,
selecting "Referred by an employee" and entering your name, so it's
attributed correctly. Referrals submitted this way are guaranteed a
response, either an interview invitation or a decline with a brief reason,
within 10 business days.

A referral is only eligible for a bonus if the candidate was not already in
Meridian's applicant pipeline, and if the candidate has not interviewed for
a Meridian role in the past 6 months.

## Bonus amounts

| Role level | Bonus |
|------------|-------|
| Individual contributor | $2,500 |
| Senior individual contributor or manager | $4,000 |
| Director and above | $6,000 |

Bonuses for hard-to-fill roles, designated by Recruiting, are increased by
$1,000 above the standard amount; these roles are flagged on the careers
portal.

## Payment timing

Half the bonus is paid in the first payroll cycle after the referred
candidate's start date. The remaining half is paid after the candidate
completes 90 days of employment, contingent on both the referrer and the
referred employee still being employed at Meridian on that date.

## Special situations

A referral for a role you are on the interview panel for is not eligible
for a bonus, to avoid a conflict of interest; see the Conflict of Interest
Policy. Referring a close relative is allowed, but the relative cannot
report to you, directly or indirectly, if hired.

## Taxes

Referral bonuses are treated as taxable income and are subject to standard
payroll withholding, the same as a regular paycheck.
