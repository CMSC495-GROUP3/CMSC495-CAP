Title: IT Acceptable Use and Security Policy
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: Information Security
Effective: 2026-03-01

## Accounts and authentication

All company systems are accessed through Okta single sign-on. Multi-factor
authentication is mandatory. An authenticator app or hardware key is required;
SMS is not an accepted second factor.

Never share credentials, including with your manager or IT. No one at Meridian
will ever ask for your password. Report any request for your password to
Information Security immediately.

Use the company password manager (1Password) for any credential that cannot be
placed behind Okta.

## Devices

Company laptops are managed and monitored for security configuration, patch
level, and malware. Meridian does not monitor personal browsing content on
company devices, but network destinations are logged.

Keep your device on a supported operating system version and install security
updates within 14 days of release. Devices more than 30 days behind are blocked
from company systems automatically.

Lock your screen when you step away. Enable full disk encryption — it is on by
default and must not be disabled.

Report a lost or stolen device to the IT Service Desk immediately, at any hour.
Remote wipe is initiated within one hour of a report.

## Personal use

Incidental personal use of company devices and networks is acceptable. Do not use
company systems for a personal business, political campaigning, or anything
illegal.

Do not store personal media libraries or personal financial records on company
devices. Company devices are wiped at separation with no opportunity to recover
personal data.

## Approved software and services

Install software from the Self Service catalog. Software outside the catalog
requires IT review — submit a request rather than installing directly.

Company data may only be placed in approved services: Google Workspace, Slack,
Workday, GitHub, Atlassian, and the approved AI assistant tenant. Do not paste
company confidential information, customer data, or employee data into
consumer AI tools, personal cloud storage, or personal email.

## Email and phishing

Meridian is targeted by phishing regularly. Verify any unexpected request to move
money, change bank details, buy gift cards, or share credentials — verify by a
channel other than the one the request arrived on.

Report suspected phishing with the Report Phish button in Gmail. Reporting a
legitimate message by mistake carries no consequence.

Simulated phishing tests are run quarterly. Employees who click are assigned a
short training module; repeated clicks trigger a conversation with the manager,
not discipline.

## Data classification

- **Public** — approved for external release.
- **Internal** — default for company information; not for external sharing.
- **Confidential** — customer data, employee data, financials, security details.
  Encrypted in transit and at rest; access on a need-to-know basis.
- **Restricted** — credentials, keys, regulated personal data. Access requires
  documented approval and is logged.

Label documents when they are Confidential or Restricted.

## Remote access

Use the company VPN on untrusted networks, including hotel, airport, and café
wifi. The VPN is not required on your registered home network.

## Offboarding

Access is revoked at the end of your final day. Return all company hardware
within 5 business days using the prepaid shipping label provided by IT.
