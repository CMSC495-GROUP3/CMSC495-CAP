Title: Jury Duty and Military Leave Policy
Category: Time Off & Leave
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Jury duty

### Eligibility and pay

All regular employees are eligible from their first day. Meridian pays your
full base salary for up to 15 business days of jury service per calendar
year. Service beyond 15 days continues, unpaid by Meridian, though you may
use PTO to cover the gap or accept the court's standard juror fee.

### Notice and documentation

Notify your manager and People Operations as soon as you receive a jury
summons, and provide the summons or a copy of it. When service concludes,
submit the court's certificate of service to People Operations so the leave
is recorded correctly.

### Returning to work

On days you're excused early or not selected for a jury, return to work for
the remainder of the day if there are at least 3 hours left in your normal
schedule. You return to the same role and responsibilities at the end of
your jury service, with no effect on performance reviews or standing.

## Military leave

### Eligibility

Employees who are members of the uniformed services, including the National
Guard and Reserves, are covered under the Uniformed Services Employment and
Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA) for military leave, training, and
reemployment.

### Pay during leave

Meridian pays the difference between your military pay and your regular
Meridian salary for up to 15 business days of military leave per calendar
year, for both annual training and activation. Leave beyond 15 days is
unpaid by Meridian, though you may elect to use PTO.

### Benefits during leave

Health coverage continues for up to 24 months of military leave under
USERRA continuation rules; for leave of 30 days or less, your coverage
continues exactly as if you were actively working, with no gap and no
change in your premium contribution.

### Reemployment rights

Employees returning from qualifying military leave are reinstated to the
position they would have held had they not taken leave, including any
intervening promotion or pay increase, provided they report back or apply
for reinstatement within the timeframe required by USERRA based on the
length of service (generally within 90 days for service of more than 180
days, shorter for shorter service).

### Notice

Provide advance notice of military service to your manager and People
Operations when possible; USERRA does not require advance notice when
military necessity prevents it.
