Title: Drug and Alcohol Policy
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Policy statement

Employees must not be impaired by alcohol, illegal drugs, or misused
prescription medication while performing work, on company premises, or
operating a vehicle on company business. This policy applies regardless of
location, including remote work during working hours.

## Alcohol at company events

Alcohol may be served at company-sponsored events such as holiday parties
and off-sites. Non-alcoholic options are always available and given equal
prominence on any drink menu or bar setup. Attendance and participation at
events involving alcohol is never required, and declining a drink carries
no consequence.

Employees are expected to drink responsibly and are personally responsible
for arranging safe transportation home; Meridian reimburses reasonable
rideshare costs from a company event through the Travel and Expense
Reimbursement Policy.

## Cannabis and state law

Cannabis remains a federally controlled substance. Regardless of its legal
status in your state, being impaired by cannabis during work hours or
reporting to work impaired is prohibited on the same basis as alcohol.
Off-duty, legal use in your own time is your own business and is not
monitored.

## Prescription and over-the-counter medication

Prescription medication taken as directed is not a policy violation, even
if it causes side effects such as drowsiness. If a medication affects your
ability to safely perform your job, such as operating machinery or driving
for work, talk to your manager or People Operations about a temporary
accommodation rather than working through the impairment.

## Testing

Meridian does not conduct random drug testing. Testing may be required
after a workplace accident where impairment is a reasonable concern, or
where required by a customer contract for on-site roles. Any such testing
is described in advance and administered by a third-party provider.

## Getting help

Meridian's Employee Assistance Program, available through the health
benefits provider, offers confidential substance use counseling and
treatment referrals at no cost. Voluntarily seeking help does not trigger
discipline. Contact information is on the benefits portal under "EAP."

## Violations

Reporting to work impaired, possessing illegal drugs on company premises, or
distributing any controlled substance at work results in immediate
termination under the Disciplinary Action and Termination Policy. A
first-time disclosure of a substance use issue, made before it results in a
workplace incident, is treated as a request for help, not a disciplinary
matter.
