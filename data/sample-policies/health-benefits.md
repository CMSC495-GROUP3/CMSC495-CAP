Title: Health Insurance and Benefits Enrollment
Category: Benefits
Owner: Benefits Team
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Coverage offered

Meridian Systems offers medical, dental, and vision coverage through Anthem.
Three medical plans are available:

- **Core PPO** — $1,500 individual deductible, 20% coinsurance after deductible.
- **Value HDHP** — $3,200 individual deductible, HSA-eligible, lowest premium.
- **Premier PPO** — $500 individual deductible, 10% coinsurance, highest premium.

Dental and vision are single-option plans. Dental covers preventive care at 100%
with no deductible, basic services at 80%, and major services at 50%, with a
$2,000 annual maximum. Vision covers one exam per year and $200 toward frames or
contacts every 24 months.

## Cost sharing

Meridian pays 85% of the employee-only premium on all medical plans and 70% of
the premium for dependents. Dental and vision are fully paid by the company for
employee-only coverage.

Premiums are deducted pre-tax over 24 pay periods.

## When you can enroll

You have three opportunities to enroll or change coverage:

1. **New hire enrollment.** You have 30 days from your start date. Coverage is
   retroactive to your first day. If you miss the 30-day window you must wait
   for open enrollment.
2. **Open enrollment.** Held annually from November 1 through November 21, with
   changes effective January 1.
3. **Qualifying life event.** Marriage, divorce, birth or adoption, loss of other
   coverage, or a dependent aging off a plan. You have 60 days from the event to
   make a change, and must provide documentation.

If you take no action as a new hire, you are not enrolled. There is no automatic
default enrollment for medical, dental, or vision.

## Health Savings Account

Employees on the Value HDHP may contribute to an HSA. Meridian contributes $750
per year for individual coverage and $1,500 for family coverage, deposited in
two installments in January and July.

The 2026 IRS contribution limits are $4,400 for individual coverage and $8,750
for family coverage, including the company contribution. Employees 55 and older
may contribute an additional $1,000.

HSA funds roll over indefinitely and remain yours if you leave the company.

## Flexible Spending Accounts

Employees on the Core or Premier PPO plans may elect a healthcare FSA up to
$3,400 per year. FSA funds do not roll over beyond a $680 carryover; plan
conservatively.

A dependent care FSA is available on all plans up to $7,500 per household.

## Dependents

Spouses, domestic partners, and children up to age 26 are eligible. Documentation
verifying the relationship is required within 30 days of adding a dependent, or
the dependent is removed retroactively.

## Questions

Plan documents live in the Benefits folder on the intranet. For claims disputes,
contact Anthem directly. For enrollment or eligibility questions, contact the
Benefits Team.
