Title: Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: Legal
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Policy statement

Meridian prohibits bribery and corruption in every form, in every country
it operates in. This policy applies to interactions with government
officials and with private-sector customers, vendors, and partners, and
complies with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and the UK Bribery Act
where applicable.

## What counts as a bribe

Anything of value, offered or given with the intent to improperly influence
a business or government decision, is a bribe. This includes cash,
gifts, excessive entertainment, a job offer to a decision-maker's relative,
or a "facilitation payment" to speed up a routine government process.
Intent matters more than the amount involved; a small payment intended to
influence a decision is still a bribe.

## Gifts and entertainment: giving

You may provide a gift or meal of reasonable, customary value to a customer
or partner as part of normal business relationship-building, up to $150 per
person per event, and not more than four times per year to the same
recipient without Legal sign-off. Never give cash or a cash equivalent, such
as a gift card, in any amount.

Anything involving a government official requires Legal approval in
advance, regardless of amount; many government ethics rules set a limit far
below Meridian's general threshold, and Legal will confirm what applies.

## Gifts and entertainment: receiving

See the Code of Conduct for the general gift limit ($100 per source per
year). Never accept anything, regardless of value, while a purchasing
decision involving that vendor is open. Report any gift offer that exceeds
these limits to your manager, who will arrange to return it or, where
returning isn't practical, donate it.

## Third parties and intermediaries

Meridian is responsible for corrupt acts committed by an agent, consultant,
or distributor acting on its behalf, even without direct knowledge.
Engaging a third party to act on Meridian's behalf in a new market requires
a due diligence review by Legal before the engagement begins, and a
contract clause prohibiting bribery on Meridian's behalf.

## Charitable and political contributions

Company funds may not be used for a political contribution or a
significant charitable donation to an organization connected to a
government official or a customer's decision-maker without Legal approval.
Personal political contributions on your own time and with your own money
are entirely your choice and are not restricted by this policy.

## Reporting

Report a suspected bribery concern to Legal or through the anonymous ethics
line at meridian.ethicspoint.com; see the Whistleblower Policy for
protection against retaliation. A violation is addressed under the
Disciplinary Action and Termination Policy and may also carry personal
legal liability for the individuals involved, independent of any action
Meridian takes.
