Title: Disciplinary Action and Termination Policy
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Approach

Meridian uses progressive discipline for most performance and conduct
issues, intended to correct behavior before it results in termination.
Progressive discipline does not apply to severe misconduct, which can result
in immediate termination on a first occurrence.

## Progressive discipline steps

1. **Verbal coaching.** Manager and employee discuss the issue directly.
   Documented in a brief note to the employee's file, not visible on a
   performance review.
2. **Written warning.** Issued when an issue repeats or does not improve
   after coaching. States the specific issue, the expected change, and the
   timeline for improvement, typically 30 to 60 days.
3. **Final written warning or performance improvement plan.** Used when a
   written warning does not resolve the issue. Sets specific, measurable
   goals and a review date, usually 30 to 90 days out. Failure to meet the
   plan's goals typically results in termination.
4. **Termination.** Used when prior steps do not resolve the issue, or
   directly for severe misconduct.

A manager may skip steps for a serious issue with People Operations
approval; skipping a step always requires that approval and is documented.

## Severe misconduct

The following typically result in immediate termination without progressive
discipline:

- Theft, fraud, or falsification of company records.
- Violence or credible threats of violence.
- Harassment or discrimination as defined in the Anti-Harassment and
  Discrimination Policy.
- Being impaired by drugs or alcohol on the job, beyond the exceptions in
  the Drug and Alcohol Policy.
- Serious breach of confidentiality or data security.

## Manager responsibilities

Consult People Operations before issuing a written warning or higher. People
Operations reviews the documentation for consistency with how similar
issues have been handled elsewhere in the company, and for compliance with
applicable law.

Document specific, observable facts, not general impressions. "Missed three
deadlines in the past month, on March 2, March 9, and March 21" is usable
documentation; "isn't a team player" is not.

## Employee rights during the process

You may bring a coworker to a disciplinary meeting as a witness, though not
as an advocate who speaks on your behalf. You have the right to respond in
writing to any written warning; your response is attached to the record
even if it does not change the outcome.

## Termination

Involuntary termination requires People Operations sign-off regardless of
level. Final pay, including accrued unused PTO, is issued according to
state law timing requirements. Benefits continuation (COBRA) information is
provided within 14 days of the termination date.

Employees terminated for performance rather than misconduct are eligible
for a neutral reference confirming title and dates of employment.

## At-will employment

Except where a written employment agreement states otherwise, employment at
Meridian is at-will: either the employee or Meridian may end the employment
relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice. This policy
describes Meridian's general practice and does not create a contractual
right to progressive discipline before termination.
