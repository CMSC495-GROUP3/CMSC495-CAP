Title: Separation and Severance Policy
Category: Employment Lifecycle
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This fictional Meridian Technologies policy covers voluntary resignation,
involuntary separation, and when severance may be offered. Severance is
discretionary unless a written agreement says otherwise. Nothing here creates an
entitlement to severance for every departure.

## Voluntary resignation

Employees should give at least two weeks' written notice to their manager and
People Operations. Meridian pays accrued unused PTO on separation where required
by the employee's work location or by written plan terms; otherwise unused PTO is
forfeited. Ask People Operations for the rule that applies to your location.

## Involuntary separation

Managers must partner with People Operations before communicating an involuntary
separation. Final pay timing follows applicable wage law for the work location.

## Severance

When Meridian offers severance, the offer is delivered in a written agreement that
states the amount, payment schedule, benefits treatment, and any release of
claims. Typical discretionary guidelines (not guarantees) are one week of base
pay per completed year of service, capped at 12 weeks, for eligible involuntary
separations not involving gross misconduct. Only the signed agreement controls.

## Return of property and access

On or before the last day, return badges, devices, and confidential materials.
System access is revoked at separation unless People Operations schedules a short
transition exception in writing.
