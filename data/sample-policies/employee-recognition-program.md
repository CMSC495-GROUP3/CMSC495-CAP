Title: Employee Recognition Program
Category: Career
Owner: People Operations
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Peer-to-peer recognition

Any employee can send a recognition through the "Kudos" app, linked from
the intranet home page, to any other employee at any time. A Kudos includes
a short note and can optionally include a small point award (25, 50, or
100 points) drawn from a monthly allowance of 300 points per employee;
unused points do not carry over to the next month.

Points accumulate in a personal account and are redeemable in the Kudos
app for gift cards, merchandise, or a donation to a charity of your choice.
Points do not expire and are not taxable income until redeemed for a cash
equivalent, at which point they are reported as supplemental wages.

## Manager-driven recognition

Managers can award a spot bonus of $100 to $500 for a specific
contribution, without needing to wait for a formal review cycle. Spot
bonuses are approved by the manager's own manager and paid in the next
payroll cycle. There's no annual limit on how many spot bonuses a manager
can award, though repeated use is reviewed by People Operations to keep
recognition meaningful and fairly distributed across a team.

## Milestone recognition

Meridian recognizes work anniversaries at 1, 3, 5, 10, and 15 years with a
card from leadership and a gift selected from a catalog, increasing in
value at each milestone. Anniversaries are announced in the
#milestones Slack channel unless the employee opts out through the Kudos
app settings.

## Quarterly and annual awards

Each quarter, People Operations runs a nomination process for a small
number of company-wide awards (such as "Above and Beyond" and "Best
Collaborator"), nominated by peers and selected by a rotating panel of
employees, not by management alone. Winners receive a $500 bonus and are
announced at the quarterly all-hands.

## Program administration

People Operations reviews recognition program usage twice a year to check
that recognition is distributed broadly across teams and levels, not
concentrated among a small group, and adjusts the point allowance or spot
bonus range if warranted.
