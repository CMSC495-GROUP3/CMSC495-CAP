Title: Social Media Policy
Category: Conduct & Compliance
Owner: Marketing
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Personal social media

You may identify as a Meridian employee on personal social media. If you do,
add a note that your views are your own and not the company's; a simple
"opinions my own" in your bio is sufficient.

Do not share confidential company information, including unreleased
products, financial results, customer names, or internal metrics, on
personal accounts. This applies even in a private or "friends only" post.

Personal social media use is your own time and judgment. Meridian does not
monitor employees' personal accounts, but conduct that violates the Code of
Conduct is addressed the same way regardless of where it occurs, including
harassment of a coworker on a personal account.

## Speaking on behalf of Meridian

Only employees authorized by Marketing may post on official Meridian
accounts or speak to press, analysts, or industry press as a company
representative. Route media inquiries to press@meridian.io rather than
responding directly.

If a journalist or analyst contacts you directly about Meridian, it is fine
to say "I'll connect you with our communications team" and forward the
request to Marketing.

## Sharing company content

You're encouraged to share, like, and comment on official Meridian posts.
When resharing, do not alter the content or add commentary that could be
read as an official company statement.

Do not share pre-release marketing materials, screenshots of unreleased
features, or internal announcements before their public release date.

## Customer and community interactions

Employees participating in public developer forums, GitHub discussions, or
support communities on Meridian's behalf should identify themselves as
Meridian employees. Escalate hostile or legal-sounding complaints to
Marketing rather than responding directly.

## Violations

Posting confidential information, harassing content, or content that
violates the Code of Conduct is handled under the Disciplinary Action and
Termination Policy, up to and including termination, regardless of whether
the post was made using a company or personal device.
